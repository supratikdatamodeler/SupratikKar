Dr. Supratik Kar Research Website

The official research and mentorship website of Dr. Supratik Kar, Associate
Professor in the Department of Chemistry and Physics and Principal Investigator
of the Chemometrics and Molecular Modeling Laboratory at Kean University, New
Jersey, USA.

The website presents Dr. Kar's research, publications, scholarly impact,
funding, awards, professional service, laboratory members, student mentorship,
and academic activities.
